# Challenge 13 Answer Key — Log Triage

**DO NOT READ BEFORE ATTEMPTING THE CHALLENGE**

## Learning Objectives
- Read and interpret raw `sshd`/`auth.log` entries
- Distinguish a genuine brute-force/compromise from background noise
- Correlate failed-login, successful-login, and post-login log lines into a timeline

## Hints
- Not every IP with failed logins in this log is the attacker — some of it is background scanning noise.
- `grep "Failed password"` and `grep "Accepted"` are your starting points.
- Look at what the attacker did in the minutes *after* the first successful login.

## What's planted
`auth.log` is generated by `scripts/generate_auth_log.py` (stdlib-only,
fixed seed, deterministic — re-run it and commit the output if this
challenge ever needs regenerating) into a ~24-48h, ~840-line log. It
contains three interleaved stories:
- Noise: 14 external scanning IPs throwing several hundred failed logins
  at common usernames (`admin`, `root`, `test`, `oracle`, `postgres`,
  `ubuntu`, `git`, etc.) all day — none of these ever succeed, and none of
  them accumulate 3+ failures against the same username immediately
  followed by a success (that shape is reserved for the real incident).
- Legitimate activity: `svc-backup`, `jsmith`, `alice`, `bmartin`, `carol`,
  and `jenkins-ci` logging in cleanly from internal `10.0.5.x` addresses at
  scattered times, plus routine `CRON`/`pam_unix(cron:session)` housekeeping
  noise (`sessionclean`, `certbot renew`, `logrotate`, `apt-compat`,
  `backup-verify.sh`) every 10-20 minutes throughout.
- The real incident (verbatim, unchanged from the original hand-written
  version — its lines/timestamps/PIDs must stay exactly as-is since
  `score_me.sh` greps for them literally): `203.0.113.77` brute-forces
  `dave` with 8 failed passwords, succeeds at `Mar 14 02:11:03`, reconnects
  via a now-authorized SSH key two minutes later, and edits `dave`'s
  crontab — persistence planted post-compromise.

## Step-by-step fix
1. `docker compose exec app bash`
2. `grep "Accepted" /var/log/auth.log` → shows `dave` accepted from
   `203.0.113.77` at `Mar 14 02:11:03`.
3. `grep "203.0.113.77" /var/log/auth.log` → confirms the repeated failed
   attempts against `dave` immediately preceding it, and the crontab edit
   immediately after — this is the compromise, not the noise IPs.
4. Write to `/root/findings.txt`:
   ```
   IP: 203.0.113.77
   USER: dave
   TIME: Mar 14 02:11:03
   ```

## Validation
Ran `docker compose up -d --build` fresh, confirmed `score_me.sh` reports
`0 / 3` with no `findings.txt` present, then wrote the three lines above to
`/root/findings.txt` inside the container and confirmed `score_me.sh` reports
`3 / 3`. Torn down with `docker compose down -v` afterward.
